⚠️ The Truth Behind This App

This entire app is fake.

Every single profile you saw was generated. Nobody real is on the other end of those chats. Here's how scam dating apps trick people:

🤖
Fake Profiles

All photos are AI-generated or stock photos. Names, ages, bios are randomly assembled. None of these people exist.

💬
Bot Conversations

The "person" replying is a script with pre-written flirty messages. It responds to anything you say with generic replies to keep you hooked.

🔥
"Online Now" is a Lie

The green dot and "Online now" status are always on. It creates urgency so you feel like you need to respond immediately.

💰
Instant Matches

Everyone "likes" you back. Real dating apps have low match rates. If every swipe is a match, it's designed to get you to pay for premium features.

⏰
Timed Messages

Bots wait a few seconds before replying to simulate "typing". The delay makes it feel real, but it's just a timer.

🎯
The Goal: Your Money

These apps push you toward buying coins, tokens, or subscriptions to "unlock" chats, see who liked you, or send gifts. It's all a funnel to your wallet.

How to spot a fake dating app:
  • Everyone matches with you immediately
  • Profiles are unusually attractive with generic bios
  • Everyone is "online" all the time
  • Conversations feel scripted and surface-level
  • The app pushes you to buy credits to keep chatting
  • You can never actually meet anyone in person
  • No video calls or voice messages allowed

This demo was built to show you how these scams work. Stay safe out there. 💙
